Added a new `ProgressBar` component, a horizontal bar for showing determinate progress or an operation of unknown duration. It supports a visible label with an optional formatted value, custom value ranges, `small`, `medium`, and `large` sizes, `primary`, `success`, `warning`, and `danger` colors, adapts its track to the surrounding background, and respects the reduced motion preference.

Affected components: ProgressBar
